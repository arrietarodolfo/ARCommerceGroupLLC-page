About AR Commerce Group LLC

Institutional Efficiency in Multichannel Wholesale & E-Commerce

Founded and operating out of Saint Johns, Florida, AR Commerce Group LLC is an agile commercial enterprise specializing in high-velocity retail distribution, marketplace expansion, and strategic brand management.

Bridging Authorized Sourcing with Modern Marketplace Fulfillment

Managed by a dedicated executive team, AR Commerce Group LLC partners directly with reputable manufacturers, master distributors, and authorized brand owners. We eliminate supply chain friction by leveraging intelligent demand forecasting and multichannel distribution networks.

Our core philosophy centers on operational transparency. In an e-commerce ecosystem frequently impacted by unauthorized third-party sellers, our team upholds rigorous Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) compliance, comprehensive brand protection, and authorized channel management across Amazon, eBay, and Walmart.

From our administrative and logistics coordination base in Saint Johns, Florida, we oversee synchronized replenishment across fulfillment centers nationwide, ensuring authentic product delivery and high consumer satisfaction ratings.
